[c/2B9CD8:CALAMITY'S VANILLA ITEM RECIPES]

This mod adds both existing AND removed recipes added by Calamity for vanilla items.
Useful whether you're doing a vanilla+ playthrough and want some of the QOL Calamity brings, or you're playing Calamity and want some of the removed recipes back.
Which recipes are added is fully customisable.

Up to date as of Calamity v2.2.2.


[c/2B9CD8:WHICH RECIPES ARE INCLUDED?]

[c/99FF99:Included:]
 - Both current recipes in Calamity, and recipes removed since v2.0.4
 - Recipes using either vanilla items or the 3 Exceptions below
 - New recipes added by Calamity for items with no vanilla recipe
 - New recipes added alongside an existing vanilla recipe

[c/FF9999:NOT included:]
 - Recipes using Calamity-exclusive items which are not one of the 3 Exceptions
 - Changes made to an existing vanilla recipe


[c/2B9CD8:EXCEPTIONS]

The following 3 Calamity items, which can be obtained in Calamity from vanilla enemies, are added by this mod and are used in their respective vanilla item recipes.

This feature is turned off by default and must be enabled in the mod's settings.

 - [c/99DCFF:Blood Orb]
   Dropped by: Blood Zombie, Drippler, The Bride, The Groom, Maggot Zombie, Clown, Wandering Eye Fish, Zombie Merman, Blood Eel, Hemogoblin Shark, Dreadnautilus
   Can also be crafted from Bloody Tear
   Used to craft: Bloody Tear, Money Trough, Vitamins, and all vanilla potions

 - [c/99DCFF:Ancient Bone Dust]
   Dropped by: Skeleton, Armored Skeleton, Skeleton Archer, Spore Skeleton, Hoplite
   Used to craft: Fledgling Wings, Armor Polish

 - [c/99DCFF:Essence of Eleum]
   Dropped by: Ice Elemental, Ice Tortoise, Icy Merman, Wolf, Ice Golem, Mister Stabby, Snow Balla, Snowman Gangsta
   Can also be found in Boreal Crates
   Used to craft: Frozen Turtle Shell
